RO-Crate describes research data and their context using JSON-LD metadata. Its graph can connect a dataset with data resources and contextual entities such as people, organizations, and processes.

[ARC RO-Crate](/details/arc-ro-crate/) uses this foundation for ARC's Publication Space. ARC profiles specify how the concepts of the [abstract model](/details/arc-data-model/) are represented in that metadata.

Find out more about RO-Crate on the [official website](https://www.researchobject.org/ro-crate/).
