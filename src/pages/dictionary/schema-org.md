Schema.org provides a vocabulary of types and properties for structured metadata. [ARC RO-Crate](/ro-crate/) profiles use schema.org terms to represent concepts from the [ARC Abstract Model](/abstract-model/).

The abstract model defines its own concepts and relationships; the publication profiles specify their concrete representation.

Find out more about schema.org on the [official website](https://schema.org/).
