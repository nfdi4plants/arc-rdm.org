DataPLANT supports research data management in plant sciences and the development of [ISA ARC](/details/isa-arc/), a reference domain implementation of ARC RDM.
Its infrastructure connects collaborative workspaces with [data publication](/details/exchange-and-publication/), which can be cited alongside a journal article or as a research output in its own right.

DataPLANT is part of the [NFDI](https://www.nfdi.de/) initiative for **data as a common good for excellent research**.
It is also known as _nfdi4plants_.
