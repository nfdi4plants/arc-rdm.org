**C**ontinuous **I**ntegration and **C**ontinuous **D**elivery (CI/CD).

In the PLANTdataHUB, automated pipelines can run selected [validation packages](/details/validation/) as an ARC changes. Their feedback concerns the evaluated state and target requirements. Passing those checks can enable a publication request; review and publication remain separate steps.

Find out more about CI/CD on [GitLab](https://about.gitlab.com/topics/ci-cd/).
