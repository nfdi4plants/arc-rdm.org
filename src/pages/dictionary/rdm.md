**Research Data Management (RDM)** covers the organization, documentation, maintenance and sharing of data and their context throughout research. It includes contributions from researchers, facility staff, computational specialists and data stewards.

[ARC RDM](/details/data-management-principle/) connects evolving collaborative [workspaces](/details/arc-workspace/) with structured descriptions for validation, exchange and [publication](/details/exchange-and-publication/). Publication releases an identifiable research state while work can continue in the workspace.
