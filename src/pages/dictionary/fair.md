Findable – Accessible – Interoperable - Reusable

Structured metadata supports these principles, while persistent identification, discovery, access and long-term availability also depend on publication services. A valid metadata representation alone does not guarantee FAIR publication. See [RDM and FAIRness](/details/fairness-and-rdm/).
