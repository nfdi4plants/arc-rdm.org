Specifications, practical guides and published work explain different parts of ARC RDM. Use the specifications for format and profile requirements, and the tool guides for release-specific instructions.

## Understand ARC RDM

Start with the [ARC RDM system overview](/details/arc-representation/) to see how its principles connect workspace and publication space. Explore each principle through [Identity by Governance](/details/identity-by-governance/), [Identity by Assignment](/details/identity-by-assignment/), and [Identity by Provenance](/details/identity-by-provenance/).

The [ISA ARC overview](/details/isa-arc/) shows how a domain implementation combines these foundations with working conventions, tools, validation and publication services.

## Foundational specifications

- [ARC Abstract Model](/abstract-model/): shared concepts for administrative, semantic and provenance descriptions.
- [ARC RO-Crate profiles](/ro-crate/): their representation and conformance requirements for exchange and publication.
- [ISA ARC Workspace](/workspace/isa-arc/): workspace organization, ISA-XLSX and WR-XLSX conventions.
