**DataPLANT's publication repository, built on InvenioRDM.** ARChive publishes selected research states as [ARC RO-Crates](/details/arc-ro-crate/) with Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs).

After target validation, researchers use [ARChigator](/details/tools-and-services/ARChigator/) to inspect the proposed contents and metadata and request publication. Reviewers can request further curation before accepting the submission. The published version remains citable while the originating workspace continues to evolve.

An ARC RO-Crate viewer provides navigable access to the publication's files and research context. References to stored large data objects allow the infrastructure to serve their contents without duplicating them for every publication.
