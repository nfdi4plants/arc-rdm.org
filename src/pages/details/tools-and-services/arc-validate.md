A CLI-based tool to **install, manage, and execute ARC validation packages**.
arc-validate is used to run ARC validation in CQC pipelines at [PLANTdataHUB]({{DATAPLANT_ARC_HUB}}).

Packages test the requirements of a selected standard, community or publication target. A passing result applies to the evaluated state and checks; it does not replace publication review. See [target-driven validation](/details/validation/).
