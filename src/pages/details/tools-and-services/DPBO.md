An intermediate ontology that acts as a broker and bridge between the individual researcher/domain experts and main ontology providers.

The [shared vocabulary guide](/details/shared-vocabulary/) explains how ontology terms give scientific attributes an explicit meaning while allowing new descriptions to develop into community conventions.
