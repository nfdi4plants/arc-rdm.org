**The predecessor of [ARCitISA](/details/tools-and-services/ARCitISA/), for managing [ISA ARC Workspaces](/details/arc-workspace/isa-arc/).** ARCitect combines workspace editing, synchronization with the [PLANTdataHUB](/details/tools-and-services/PLANTdataHUB/), and ontology-assisted annotation through [Swate](/details/tools-and-services/SWATE/).

Existing ARCitect tutorials and manuals describe this application. For current development and installation guidance, see [ARCitISA and the transition notes](https://nfdi4plants.github.io/nfdi4plants.knowledgebase/arcitisa/release-note/).
