The [Central Data Facility (CDF)](https://cdf.uni-freiburg.de/en/about.html) supports researchers at the University of Freiburg with research data management, from planning and storage to analysis, collaboration, and publication. Its knowledge base connects researchers with guidance, training, and services across the university.

CDF's service catalogue presents **ARC Data Hub** as an environment for collaborative research data management and publication. The described DataPLANT infrastructure brings together GitLab, InvenioRDM, and workflow systems such as Galaxy. It supports teams in annotating data, maintaining revision histories, sharing research resources, and preparing publications. Storage integration accommodates large datasets, while APIs and webhooks connect the platform to research workflows and automated pipelines. InvenioRDM provides the publication component for datasets with persistent identifiers.

Read the [CDF ARC Data Hub service description](https://cdf.uni-freiburg.de/en/rdm-catalogue/arc-datahub.html).
