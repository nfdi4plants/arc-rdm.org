In the Collaborative Research Centre 1535 “Microbial networking – from organelles to cross-kingdom communities” (MibiNet), Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf, RWTH Aachen University, Bielefeld University, the University of Cologne, Forschungszentrum Jülich and the Max Planck Institute for Plant Breeding Research study how microorganisms interact and exchange nutrients and information. The research spans intracellular interactions of organelles and endosymbionts with their host cells as well as interactions within cross-kingdom microbial communities, with the goal of understanding how organelles evolve and how microbiome functions emerge.

> Interdisciplinary collaboration across many projects is central to MibiNet, and it produces a wide range of data types, including cultivation data, microscopy, sequencing, metabolomics and modelling. MibiNet uses **ARCs** as the format for **data publication**, giving all of these data types one common structure that keeps the full experimental context. To connect the lab bench and data publication without manual re-entry, MibiNet developed **elab2ARC**, a tool that transfers protocols and metadata from the electronic lab notebook eLabFTW directly into ARCs. Teaching and training in research data management and ARC usage are part of MibiNet’s program for early career researchers, which helps make ARCs part of everyday research practice.
